# Community House Website Redesign

Welcome to the repository for the Community House website improvement project. This project aims to build a clearer, more modern and more functional web presence for [Community House](https://communityhouse.org.za/), a heritage site, labour and community history museum, and home to 24 organisations in Salt River, Cape Town.

## 🎯 Project Scope

Based on our review of the current website (see `docs/Community_House_Website_Recommendations.pdf`), the scope includes:

*   **Clearer Homepage:** A hero section that explains what Community House is, who is based there and what visitors can do, with a short About statement and clear buttons (Book a Venue, Visit, Donate).
*   **Simpler Structure:** Give each topic one page and one menu item (Book a Venue, Donate/Get Involved, Gallery, Tenants, History, Contact) instead of repeating donation and venue hire across the homepage.
*   **Working Features:** Fix the "Call Us Today" button, the social media icons, the Gallery page (it currently shows `[instagram-feed feed=1]`) and the Book a Venue calendar.
*   **Fresh Content and Images:** Replace outdated 2020 photos, fix uneven image layouts and update the TikTok/social feed.
*   **Readability and Accessibility:** Larger, higher-contrast text on the History and Services sections, clearer contact form labels, and allow zooming on phones (WCAG).
*   **Mobile Responsiveness:** Make sure the site works on mobile, tablet and desktop.

> Note: these are suggestions to discuss with Community House. Nothing is final until they approve it.
